Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Theos AI ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit theosai.net or get in touch with us. We keep this simple: we collect only what we need to respond to you and do our work, and we don't sell your data.

Theos AI is an AI implementation business. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at jed@theosai.net.

2. Information we collect

No tracking cookies or analytics. This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party marketing pixels. We don't build advertising profiles about you.

  • To respond to your inquiries and communicate with you;

4. How your information is shared

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate, and only as needed:

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above — for example, to maintain our correspondence and any client relationship — and then delete or anonymize it, unless a longer period is required by law.

Our website and services are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.
