Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This policy explains how Theos Gaming (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, purchase our products, join our mailing list, or contact us.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address, phone number, and shipping or billing address.
  • Order information, including products purchased, order history, returns, and support requests.
  • Payment information, processed securely by Shopify and our payment providers. We do not directly store complete card details.
  • Device and usage information, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and interactions with the store.
  • Marketing preferences, including whether you have asked to receive product drops and other updates.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • process, fulfill, deliver, and support orders;
  • communicate about purchases, returns, warranties, or support requests;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the store;
  • prevent fraud, misuse, and other harmful activity;
  • send marketing communications when you have opted in; and
  • meet legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Our store and service providers may use cookies and similar technologies needed for the cart, checkout, account security, preferences, analytics, and store performance. Where required, you can manage non-essential cookies through the consent controls shown on the store or through your browser settings.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with providers that help us run the store, including:

  • Shopify, which hosts and powers our ecommerce services;
  • payment processors and fraud-prevention providers;
  • shipping, fulfillment, customer support, email, and analytics providers;
  • professional advisers and authorities where required by law; and
  • a successor organization in connection with a merger, financing, sale, or restructuring.

Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy practices. Shopify’s privacy information is available at shopify.com/legal/privacy.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including to fulfill orders, provide warranty support, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting requirements.

6. Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in an email. To submit another request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

7. Security

We use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No online service or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Where required, we and our providers use recognized legal safeguards for international transfers.

9. Children

Our store is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our store, providers, or legal obligations. We will revise the date above when changes are published.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@theosgaming.com.
