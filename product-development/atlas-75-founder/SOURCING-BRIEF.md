# Atlas 75 HE Founder’s Edition — sourcing brief

Status: supplier sourcing  
Market: United States  
Total cash cap: **$1,000, including samples, freight, first inventory, and local branding**

## Product position

The first Atlas 75 will be a **Theos-selected and tuned Founder’s Edition** built
on an existing production keyboard platform. It will not be described as
engineered from scratch. Theos will own the product selection, acceptance
testing, visual treatment, packaging experience, support, and final claims.

The first batch is a validation batch, not mass production. No unit moves to the
store as available inventory until a representative sample passes the acceptance
plan and the supplier’s compliance documents are checked.

## Required platform

| Area | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Layout | ANSI 75% or close equivalent, dedicated arrows, compact navigation column |
| Switch sensing | Hall-effect magnetic switches |
| Performance controls | Adjustable per-key actuation and rapid trigger |
| Connection | USB-C wired is mandatory; low-latency 2.4 GHz is preferred only with evidence; Bluetooth is optional |
| Rollover | Full-key rollover in wired mode |
| Profiles | At least one onboard profile; three preferred |
| Software | Current, downloadable configurator with reset and firmware-recovery path |
| Case | Stock black or dark graphite; no permanent third-party logo on the top surface |
| Keycaps | Replaceable MX-compatible caps; north-facing interference must be disclosed |
| Lighting | Disable-able RGB or restrained white/orange mode |
| Included | Cable, receiver when wireless, keycap/switch tool, basic documentation |
| US support | Supplier provides firmware files, defect handling, and spare-unit terms in writing |

## Supplier and order gates

The candidate must:

- already be in production;
- permit a two-unit sample order;
- support a stock-unit order of no more than ten units;
- identify the legal supplier and manufacturing location;
- provide an invoice and written defect/replacement terms;
- state whether the hardware and firmware may legally be resold under a Theos
  edition name;
- provide model-matched FCC evidence for any radio, plus CE/RoHS and battery
  UN 38.3 documentation where applicable;
- disclose every existing logo on the enclosure, keycaps, boot screen,
  configurator, receiver, cable, and packaging; and
- keep the estimated landed keyboard cost at or below **$70 per sellable unit**
  for the founder batch.

## Immediate disqualifiers

- A supplier asks to place an FCC/CE logo without a model-matched report.
- The sample and production unit use different PCB, firmware, switches, or case.
- Wireless is advertised without an FCC identifier or supporting grantee/model.
- Software is supplied only as an unsigned file-share executable.
- Existing branding would need to be hidden or misrepresented.
- The supplier refuses to identify the factory or legal seller on the invoice.
- MOQ exceeds the available budget.
- Performance claims cannot be disabled until measured.
- The board cannot be reset or recovered after a failed firmware update.

## Founder-batch budget caps

These are spending gates, not supplier quotes.

| Item | Maximum |
| --- | ---: |
| Two platform samples | $180 |
| Sample and batch freight/duty buffer | $120 |
| Six to eight sellable stock keyboards | $500 |
| Local accent keys, labels, sleeves, inserts, and cartons | $120 |
| Defect/contingency reserve | $80 |
| **Total** | **$1,000** |

If samples consume more than $300 delivered, the initial sellable quantity must
fall; the compliance and contingency reserves must not be removed to compensate.

## Branding plan within this budget

Factory logo customization is optional and will be rejected if it creates a
large MOQ or setup fee. The founder batch can be made distinct locally with:

- one orange escape key and a small set of warm-white/orange accent keys;
- a removable underside serialized Theos edition label;
- a fitted black packaging sleeve;
- a Theos quick-start, calibration, and warranty card;
- a braided orange/black cable only if electrically equivalent and properly
  rated; and
- a saved default profile after supplier permission and repeatability checks.

No third-party trademark will be covered, removed, or relabeled without written
authorization.

## Sample acceptance gate

A sample must pass:

1. visual inspection and model/BOM reconciliation;
2. every-key registration and calibration at minimum, midpoint, and maximum
   actuation settings;
3. rapid-trigger repeatability and chatter checks;
4. full-key rollover and shortcut testing;
5. wired sleep/wake, reconnect, reset, and firmware-recovery tests;
6. wireless pairing, congestion, reconnect, and runtime checks when applicable;
7. configurator install, malware scan, profile save/export, and clean uninstall;
8. stabilizer, case resonance, connector strain, and desk-flex inspection;
9. two-meter cable movement and receiver-range checks where applicable;
10. packaging completeness and two repeated unpack/repack cycles; and
11. document matching for exact model, radio module, battery, and legal supplier.

Only observed results may become storefront claims.

## Decision sequence

1. Verify supplier identity and listing evidence.
2. Send the same RFQ to at least three candidates.
3. Reject incomplete compliance responses before sample payment.
4. Buy no more than two competing samples.
5. Test both against one scorecard.
6. Select one platform or stop the project.
7. Confirm the six-to-eight-unit landed quote and replacement allowance.
8. Prepare local branding on one production-representative unit.
9. Photograph the approved physical unit.
10. Create a Shopify draft product with measured specifications only.
