# Atlas 75 HE Founder’s Edition — US compliance gate

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. A small founder batch is not
automatically exempt from US radio, import, transport, warranty, or product-safety
obligations.

## Do not advertise or take orders until these gates clear

### 1. Radio and digital-device authorization

A 2.4 GHz/Bluetooth keyboard is an intentional radiator. FCC rules generally
require authorization, labeling, and user information before a radiofrequency
device is imported, advertised, or sold in the United States.

Required evidence:

- exact FCC ID printed on or electronically available for the production unit;
- FCC grant with matching grantee, model/family, frequency bands, and equipment class;
- internal/external photographs or model-difference letter connecting the exact
  sample to the certified hardware;
- Part 15 user statements and label location;
- evidence for the keyboard and any separately transmitting receiver; and
- Supplier’s Declaration of Conformity information for applicable digital-device
  functions, including the US responsible party.

The importer of record can become the US responsible party for equipment using
the SDoC procedure and may need to retain test records and provide equipment to
the FCC on request.

Sources:

- [FCC equipment authorization procedures](https://www.fcc.gov/general/equipment-authorization-procedures)
- [FCC marketing and importation enforcement](https://www.fcc.gov/enforcement/areas/equipment-authorization-marketing-importation)
- [FCC importation guidance](https://www.fcc.gov/oet/ea/importation)

### 2. Lithium-battery transport

For a wireless model, obtain the complete UN 38.3 test summary for the exact
battery design, not a one-line “UN 38.3 compliant” statement. Confirm that the
cell/battery model in the summary matches the sample and production units.

Theos must be able to make the test summary available as a subsequent
distributor. The shipper remains responsible for offering a tested battery for
transport and for compliant packaging/documentation.

Required evidence:

- UN 38.3 test summary containing all required elements;
- current SDS/MSDS;
- cell and battery pack manufacturer/model;
- watt-hour rating and pack configuration;
- air/ground shipping instructions; and
- confirmation that supplier freight includes lawful battery handling.

Sources:

- [PHMSA lithium battery test summary guide](https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/sites/phmsa.dot.gov/files/2024-09/Lithium-Battery-Test-Summary-2024.pdf)
- [PHMSA lithium battery guide](https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/sites/phmsa.dot.gov/files/2024-11/Lithium-Battery-Guide-2024.pdf)

### 3. Country of origin and importer identity

The production unit or permitted container must remain legibly marked with the
actual country of origin. Local keycaps, labels, sleeves, testing, or profile
configuration do not normally turn an imported finished keyboard into a US-made
product.

The site and package must not imply “Made in USA.” If another geographic name
could confuse the purchaser, use clear origin wording in the required proximity.

Source:

- [CBP country-of-origin marking guidance](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/rulings/informed-compliance-publications/marking-country-origin-us-imports)

### 4. Product safety and incident records

Create a serial/batch register and preserve supplier, sample, inspection, defect,
support, and battery records. Importers, distributors, and retailers have
independent CPSC reporting duties when information reasonably indicates a
defect, substantial product hazard, unreasonable risk, or noncompliance.

Required operations:

- support channel capable of identifying batch and serial;
- written incident/escalation procedure;
- retained complaint photos/videos and corrective actions;
- supplier recall/contact commitment; and
- product-liability insurance review before sale.

Sources:

- [CPSC duty to report](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Recall-Guidance/Duty-to-Report-to-the-CPSC-Your-Rights-and-Responsibilities)
- [CPSC retailer responsibilities](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Business-Guidance/Retailers-Product-Safety-and-Your-Responsibilities)

### 5. Warranty and returns

If Theos offers a written consumer warranty, make the terms available before
purchase, title it “full” or “limited” as applicable, and state the coverage in
one clear document. Do not use a blanket “warranty void if removed” or require
paid branded parts/service unless counsel confirms an allowed exception.

The supplier’s retail warranty may not cover units bought through a reseller.
The Theos budget therefore needs a replacement/defect reserve and written
supplier remedies.

Source:

- [FTC Businessperson’s Guide to Federal Warranty Law](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law)

### 6. Brand, software, and content rights

Before using “Atlas 75 HE” commercially:

- complete a federal, state, common-law, domain, marketplace, and product-name
  clearance search with qualified counsel;
- obtain written permission for all manufacturer product photographs, manuals,
  diagrams, software screenshots, and marks used on the Theos site;
- state the original manufacturer/brand where needed to avoid source confusion;
- obtain written rights to redistribute or link the configurator and firmware;
- document whether customer profiles/macros create any data collection; and
- do not cover, remove, or replace another party’s required or trademarked label.

USPTO explains that an exact-match search alone is not comprehensive:

- [USPTO federal trademark searching guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching)

## Documents required before sample payment

Commercial sample payment can proceed only after receiving:

1. legal supplier name, registration, address, and matching invoice entity;
2. exact model, PCB/firmware revision, switch, radio, receiver, and battery;
3. full delivered sample quote and payment-protection terms;
4. FCC ID/grant and model-link evidence;
5. CE/RoHS reports for the exact family;
6. UN 38.3 test summary and battery SDS/MSDS when wireless;
7. origin and regulatory label photographs;
8. firmware/configurator download and recovery documentation;
9. written stock-unit resale/co-branding and image-use permission;
10. written DOA/defect replacement terms; and
11. confirmation that sample and batch use the same production revision.

Generic certificates, expired links, edited screenshots, or reports for a
different board do not clear this gate.

## Documents required before the Shopify product leaves draft

- Passed two-sample test report and photo set.
- Final invoice and landed-cost calculation.
- Batch/serial and inspection records.
- Approved product and package labels.
- Limited warranty and returns terms.
- Customer support and incident-escalation process.
- Licensed real product photography.
- Claim-evidence table mapping every storefront statement to observation,
  manufacturer documentation, or independent testing.
- Inventory physically received and counted.
