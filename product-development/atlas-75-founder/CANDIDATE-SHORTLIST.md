# Atlas 75 HE Founder’s Edition — candidate shortlist

Research snapshot: August 25, 2026  
Status: official inquiries sent; no sample purchase approved

Prices below are public listing prices, not accepted wholesale quotes. Freight,
duty, defects, and local packaging remain unquoted.

## Current recommendation

Do not spend the sample budget until at least one official channel returns the
model-matched compliance package and written resale/co-branding terms.

The preferred sample sequence is:

1. **AULA F75 HE**, if AULA supplies the FCC/battery documents and low-volume
   written terms;
2. **AJAZZ AK820 Max HE tri-mode**, if AJAZZ confirms production availability
   and that its FCC evidence applies to the exact HE radio revision; then
3. **GamaKay TK75HE V2** only if its official team supplies missing FCC and
   battery evidence.

YUNZII RT80 is technically attractive but its official page is sold out and its
current public price consumes too much of the founder-batch budget unless a
direct distributor quote changes the economics.

## Required response before winner selection

For any candidate to win, its official channel must return:

1. exact in-stock model/revision and black US ANSI photos;
2. delivered pricing for two samples and six/eight stock units;
3. written resale/co-branding and product-image permission;
4. FCC chain for keyboard and receiver;
5. CE/RoHS, complete UN 38.3 summary, and battery SDS;
6. configurator/firmware/recovery access;
7. written defect replacement terms; and
8. confirmation that samples and batch units are the same production revision.

Until then, there is a real shortlist—not an approved Theos product.
