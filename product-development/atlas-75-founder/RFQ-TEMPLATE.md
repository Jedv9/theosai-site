## Quote normalization

Convert each reply to:

```text
sample delivered total
= samples + sample freight + tax/duty/fees

batch landed total
= units + batch freight + tax/duty + inspection + local packaging

estimated landed unit
= batch landed total / sellable units
```

Do not compare one supplier’s EXW number to another supplier’s DDP number.

## Stop conditions during negotiation

- Supplier moves payment outside the verified marketplace before trust is established.
- Invoice entity differs from the verified supplier without a documented relationship.
- Model number is removed from certificates or attachments.
- “Certificate available after order” is the only compliance response.
- Sample revision cannot be guaranteed for the batch.
- Firmware/configurator download is inaccessible before payment.
- Supplier pressures for a deposit before providing a complete quote and identity.
